Child Safety Standards

PawsCare & Kinni’s 2nd Home is committed to protecting children and has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE/CSAM).

Our app does not allow any content that involves sexual exploitation, abuse, or endangerment of minors. Any such content, if identified, is immediately removed and reported to the appropriate authorities in accordance with applicable laws.

Users can report child safety concerns or inappropriate content by contacting us via email. All reports are reviewed promptly and appropriate action is taken.

We cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies and comply with all relevant child protection and online safety regulations.

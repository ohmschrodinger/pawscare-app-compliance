Account Deletion Instructions

Users can permanently delete their PawsCare account directly from within the mobile application by following the steps below.

  1. Open the PawsCare mobile application.
  2. Navigate to the Settings page.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap on Delete Account.
  4. Carefully read the deletion warning shown on the screen.
  5. Type delete in the confirmation field.
  6. Tap the Delete button to confirm.
Important:

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once confirmed, your account and profile are deleted instantly.

The following data will be permanently removed:

  • Profile and personal information
  • Saved posts and favorites
